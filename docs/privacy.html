Privacy Policy — Hisaab Sakhi

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Hisaab Sakhi ("the app", "we", "us") is a household expense-tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What We Collect

Stored only on your device (never leaves your phone):

  • All expense, income, and savings entries you add
  • Your language and currency preferences

Sent to our servers — only if you say yes:

On first launch, and any time after in Settings, you choose whether to share data for analytics. If you agree, we receive:

  • Expense amount, category, and date
  • Income amount, source, and date
  • Savings goal names and amounts
  • Approximate city-level location (only if you separately grant location permission)
  • A randomly generated device identifier (not linked to your name, phone number, or any account)

We never collect your name, phone number, email, bank account or card details, or precise GPS location.

Optional purchase: The app offers a one-time, optional "support the developer" purchase handled entirely by Google Play's billing system. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made — never your payment details. This purchase never unlocks or gates any feature.

Why We Collect It

To build aggregated, anonymized insights into household spending patterns (e.g. "average grocery spend this month across users in a city"). Individual entries are never shown to anyone, published, or sold as identifiable records.

Your Choices

  • Decline or withdraw consent anytime in Settings → "Share data for analytics." The app works fully offline either way — declining does not limit any feature.
  • Location is requested separately from the main consent, only after you opt into data sharing, and only for city-level tagging.
  • Withdrawing consent stops all future syncing immediately; it does not delete data already sent to our servers. To request deletion of previously synced data, contact us below.

Data Retention & Deletion

Data you've synced is retained for as long as your consent is active. To request deletion of your synced data, email us at aayushpursnani024@gmail.com with your device identifier (found in Settings) or simply describe your request — we will process it within a reasonable time and confirm once complete.

Data Storage & Security

Synced data is stored on servers hosted in Singapore (Supabase). We use standard security practices (encrypted connections, access controls) to protect it, but no system is 100% secure.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
